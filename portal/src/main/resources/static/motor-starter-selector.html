      Selection references

      Manufacturer and industry guides underpin the screening logic. Use current instructions for the *exact product*, motor winding and applicable Canadian installation jurisdiction:

      • Schneider Electric — motor starting methods (DOL, wye–delta and part-winding).
      • Rockwell Automation — PowerFlex low-voltage drive selection (normal/heavy-duty output ratings).
      • ASHRAE — motors, motor controls and variable-frequency drives (single-phase starting mechanisms).

      Do not assume motor protection values from generic percentages. Verify CEC 2024 / applicable OESC Section 28 and associated rules, equipment listing and the local authority having jurisdiction.
